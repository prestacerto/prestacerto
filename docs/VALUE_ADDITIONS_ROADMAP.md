# PrestaCerto - Value Additions Roadmap 🎯

## Estratégia: De Marketplace → Ecosystem

Agregar valor em **4 dimensões**: Confiança, Produtividade, Learning, Community

---

## 🔐 TRUST & VERIFICATION (Tier 1 - Máxima Urgência)

### 1. **Identity Verification**
**Why:** Fraude é #1 pain point em marketplaces  
**How:** 
- CPF/CNPJ verification (integração com via.com.br)
- Phone verification badge
- Email verification
- LinkedIn profile linking

**Monetize:** R$ 19/verificação (freelancer), R$ 99 (cliente Premium)  
**Revenue Potential:** R$ 30-50k/mês @ 1500 users

### 2. **Enhanced Reviews & Ratings**
**Why:** Ratings atuais são simples demais  
**How:**
- 5 categorias de avaliação (Qualidade, Comunicação, Prazo, Preço, Profissionalismo)
- Foto da entrega obrigatória
- Revisão de reviews (moderação)
- Seller response template

**Monetize:** Incluído em Pro  
**Revenue Impact:** +15% LTV (confiança = higher spend)

### 3. **Guarantees & Insurance**
**Why:** Clientes querem proteção  
**How:**
- Escrow automático (Presta holds money)
- Dispute resolution (IA + human review)
- Refund protection (R$ 0-5000)
- Performance guarantee (remake if unhappy)

**Monetize:** 2-3% taxa no projeto  
**Revenue Potential:** R$ 40-100k/mês @ escala

## ⚡ PRODUCTIVITY TOOLS (Tier 2 - 30 dias)

### 4. **Time Tracking & Billing**
**Why:** Freelancers perdem tempo rastreando horas  
**How:**
- Desktop app com timer integrado
- Screenshot monitoring (optional)
- Automatic invoice generation
- Export para contabilidade (opcional)

**Monetize:** R$ 29/mês (freelancer)  
**Revenue Potential:** R$ 20-40k/mês @ 700 users

### 5. **Contract Generator**
**Why:** Contratos customizados por categoria = profissionalismo  
**How:**
- Templates legais por setor (Web Dev, Design, etc)
- Auto-fill com dados do projeto
- E-signature integration
- Multi-language

**Monetize:** R$ 9 por contrato OU R$ 49/mês unlimited  
**Revenue Potential:** R$ 15-30k/mês

### 6. **Invoice & Accounting Integration**
**Why:** Freelancers odeiam contas  
**How:**
- Auto-generate invoices
- Integração com NotaFiscal eletrônica
- Sync com Wise/PayPal para pagamentos
- Integração Zapier (enviar pra Nuvem Shop, etc)

**Monetize:** R$ 39/mês (Premium)  
**Revenue Impact:** +R$ 25-40k/mês

### 7. **Project Management Light**
**Why:** Comunicação dentro do Presta = stickiness  
**How:**
- Kanban simples (To-Do, In Progress, Done)
- File sharing (Supabase Storage)
- Comments & notifications
- Milestone tracking

**Monetize:** Free (engagement hook) + R$ 19/mês for Teams  
**Revenue Impact:** Retention +20%

## 📚 LEARNING & CERTIFICATION (Tier 3 - Gamification)

### 8. **Skill Academy (Premium Courses)**
**Why:** Upskilling = retention + income = more projects  
**How:**
- Curated courses por skill (Web Dev 101, etc)
- Video + projects + quizzes
- Partner with Udemy creators (revenue share)
- Certificate for CV

**Monetize:** R$ 49-199 por curso, 70/30 split  
**Revenue Potential:** R$ 50-100k/mês @ scaled

### 9. **AI-Powered Skill Recommendations**
**Why:** Freelancers don't know what to learn next  
**How:**
- Analyze portfolio + completed projects
- "You're 80% complete in React, learn TypeScript next"
- Suggest based on trending skills

**Monetize:** Included in Pro  
**Revenue Impact:** +10% upsell to Business

### 10. **Certification Marketplace**
**Why:** Sell certifications other platforms designed  
**How:**
- Partner with Coursera, LinkedIn Learning
- White-label their certs with Presta brand
- Revenue share model

**Monetize:** 30-40% commission on referrals  
**Revenue Potential:** R$ 20-50k/mês

## 👥 COMMUNITY & NETWORKING (Tier 4 - Long-term)

### 11. **Mastermind Groups**
**Why:** Network effect = stickiness  
**How:**
- Private groups por skill (Web Devs, Designers, etc)
- Monthly group calls (video)
- Resource sharing
- Accountability partners

**Monetize:** R$ 29-79/mês per group  
**Revenue Potential:** R$ 30-60k/mês @ 100 groups

### 12. **Success Stories & Case Studies**
**Why:** Social proof = conversion  
**How:**
- Showcase top freelancers (with their permission)
- "How I made R$ 50k in 3 months" interviews
- Video testimonials
- Published on blog + LinkedIn

**Monetize:** Free (brand value + engagement)  
**Revenue Impact:** +20% CAC efficiency

### 13. **Referral Network (B2B)**
**Why:** Agencies need to expand capacity  
**How:**
- Agency dashboard (see freelancers, share projects)
- Revenue share (30%) on projects referred
- White-label for large agencies

**Monetize:** 30% commission on referred projects  
**Revenue Potential:** R$ 100-300k/mês @ scaled

## 🤖 AI & AUTOMATION (Tier 5 - Differentiation)

### 14. **AI Project Matcher**
**Why:** Right freelancer for right project = higher success  
**How:**
- ML model: project desc → top 5 matches
- Freelancer accepts/rejects (training data)
- Auto-notify matching freelancers
- Proposal quality scoring

**Monetize:** Included in Pro  
**Revenue Impact:** +15% acceptance rate = +R$ 10-20k/mth

### 15. **AI Proposal Coach**
**Why:** Better proposals = higher win rate  
**How:**
- "Improve your proposal in 30 seconds"
- Rewrite suggestions (already have Certo AI)
- Price negotiation suggestions
- Response time recommendations

**Monetize:** R$ 19/mês or tokens (50 rewrites/mth)  
**Revenue Potential:** R$ 15-30k/mês

### 16. **Trend Forecasting**
**Why:** Freelancers want to know what skills are hot  
**How:**
- ML on Presta data (project volume trends)
- "Demand for React in 2026: ↑12% YoY"
- Skill scarcity index
- Regional trends

**Monetize:** R$ 49/mês (Business tier)  
**Revenue Impact:** Premium feature, +R$ 15-25k/mth

## 💳 FINANCIAL SERVICES (Tier 6 - Scaling)

### 17. **Built-in Payments (Wallets)**
**Why:** Freelancers earn → keep money on Presta → spend on more services  
**How:**
- Presta wallet (instead of Pix-only)
- Float money between jobs
- Earn interest on balance (0.5% APY)
- Use credits to buy services

**Monetize:** 1% float on balances + interest margin  
**Revenue Potential:** R$ 50-150k/mês @ scale

### 18. **Micro-Loans for Freelancers**
**Why:** Freelancers need cash flow smoothing  
**How:**
- Loan against future earnings
- R$ 500-10k available
- 2% monthly interest
- Auto-repay from projects

**Monetize:** 2-5% interest margin  
**Revenue Potential:** R$ 100-300k/mês @ scale

### 19. **Payment Plans (BNPL)**
**Why:** High-ticket projects need financing  
**How:**
- Client can split R$ 10k project into 3 payments
- Presta finances it
- Freelancer gets paid upfront

**Monetize:** 2-3% fee to client  
**Revenue Potential:** R$ 30-80k/mês
